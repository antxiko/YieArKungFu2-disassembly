The game checks Yie Ar Kung-Fu (RC-725) in the second slot. Before it even installs the interrupt hook it scans the four slots, switches page 1 of each and takes two 16-byte sums; if they come out right, from level 3 on a drink shows up that refills your bar when both fighters are nearly out. And the soup that makes you invulnerable does not come out on its own: you have to strike at a different spot in every round, the eight in the table at 0x507A.

Konami - (c) Konami 1985 - An RC-737 32 KB cartridge - MSX1 - pages 1 and 2 - Dump bcb41b35...

Not one capture here. Every picture is drawn from the bytes of the ROM, by running in Python the very same scripts, mirrors and figure readers the Z80 runs, and then checked byte for byte against openMSX's VRAM: nine screens dumped from the emulator come out with zero differences in colour (6,144 bytes), patterns (6,144) and sprite patterns (1,792), and the title screen matches 768 of 768 tiles as well. The listing and the numbers are reproducible with make, and reassembling gives back the ROM byte for byte.
